What can / can't I send?
Commonly accepted items:
• Clothing & shoes
• Jewelry & watches
• Cell phones & small electronics
• Documents, books, paperwork
• Cosmetics (non-aerosol, small quantities)
• Accessories, small gifts
• Hair tools (without battery)

• Hair extension (wigs, frontals)

Package limits:
• Maximum weight: **8 kg** (to fit safely in traveler luggage)
Not allowed (safety & airline rules):
• Liquids / gels > 100 ml
• Perishable food or plants
• Weapons, ammunition, explosives
• Hazardous materials (batteries >100Wh without approval, chemicals)
• Illegal substances or counterfeit goods, drugs or controlled subtances
• Large/heavy items (>8 kg or oversized)

If your item is not listed, choose “Other item” and describe it clearly.
All requests are reviewed for compliance.
